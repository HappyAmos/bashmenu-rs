# BashMenu-RS Community Guidelines & Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

We are dedicated to providing a welcoming, productive, and harassment-free environment for all contributors and maintainers, regardless of background, experience level, identity, or perspective.

## Expectations & Standards

We ask that all participants in the BashMenu-RS community uphold the following principles:

- **Respect and Professionalism:** Treat fellow contributors with kindness and professional courtesy. Focus critiques on code, architecture, and technical merits rather than individuals.
- **Collaborative Problem Solving:** Assume good intent. Offer constructive, actionable feedback during reviews and technical discussions.
- **Thoughtful Craftsmanship:** Respect the project's technical goals—including high-performance terminal UX, zero-latency startup, and terminal state safety—while helping others understand these invariants.
- **Integrity and Inclusivity:** Ensure that discussions, pull requests, issue trackers, and community spaces remain inclusive and accessible to everyone.

## Unacceptable Behavior

The following behaviors are incompatible with participation in this project:

- Personal insults, derogatory commentary, or offensive language.
- Harassment, trolling, or persistent unwanted contact.
- Disruption of community workflows, discussions, or issue threads.
- Any form of discriminatory conduct or speech.

## Enforcement & Reporting

Project maintainers are responsible for maintaining a constructive environment and reserve the right to remove or moderate comments, close issues or pull requests, or suspend access for conduct that violates these standards.
